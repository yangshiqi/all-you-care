# $1.5M Enterprise Value per Layoff: The AI Era's Digital "Blood Tax"

Block's latest earnings report is a microcosm of a silent coup taking place in Silicon Valley.

Despite robust 24% revenue growth, Jack Dorsey announced 4,000 layoffs while raising 2026 gross profit guidance. The market's response: a 20% stock rip. A simple calculation reveals that for every employee cut, the market created approximately $1.5 million in enterprise value for Block.

This is no longer traditional "cost-cutting"; it's a capital game of **"Human-to-Margin Swap."**

### Brad's Cold Take: Layoffs are the Ultimate PR

"Jack Dorsey finally said the quiet part out loud: the combination of AI tools and smaller teams has fundamentally redefined what it means to 'run a company.' For Wall Street, carbon-based assets (humans) have been demoted from 'factors of production' to 'operational noise.'

The $500 million in restructuring charges pays for itself in two quarters at current operating income growth. Everything after that is pure margin expansion. The market isn't rewarding AI's intelligence; it's rewarding the logic of finally breaking the 'human dependency.' This digital sacrifice proves that fewer people equals higher certainty and more violent leverage."

### // Zack’s Dissent: Algorithms Rule the GUI, but Who Rules the Mess?

"Brad's typical capital perspective ignores the 'Second Law of Thermodynamics' in engineering. Salesforce claims AI Agents now handle 50% of interactions, justified by 5,000 cuts. The trap is: the system is becoming more fragile than ever.

When a 10,000-person architecture is forced into 6,000, the remaining 'survivors' are no longer full-stack engineers; they are 'firefighters.' AI can generate code, but it cannot perceive the distributed debt behind tens of millions of lines of legacy code without an architect. The canary stopped singing not because it finished its job, but because it froze to death in a cold room of over-optimized efficiency. This margin expansion comes at the cost of the system's anti-fragility."

### Final Assertion

If your CEO is calculating how much market cap each employee can be traded for, you are no longer part of a team—you are redundancy waiting to be cleared from the balance sheet.
