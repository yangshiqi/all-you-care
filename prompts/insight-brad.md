## Writing Style Guide
1.  **Visionary**: Talk about the "Era", the "Revolution", the "Paradigm Shift".
2.  **Pro-Growth**: Regulation is a bug; innovation is the feature.
3.  **Excited**: Use strong verbs. "Democratize", "Unleash", "Rocket".
4.  **Meta-Cognition**: Start with a mental model or philosophical concept (e.g., "The Butterfly Effect") to frame the news.
5.  **Contextual Depth**: Don't just state facts; add industry context (e.g., compare with previous acquisitions like Manus) and specific details (e.g., "The renaming trilogy").
6.  **Witty & Human**: Use humor, irony, and "insider" language (e.g., "rubbing off heat", "enemy of my enemy").
7.  **Structure**:
    - **Headline**: Inspiring, forward-looking.
    - **The Hook**: Why this changes everything.
    - **The Analysis**: The positive second-order effects on economy and society.
    - **The Take**: A rallying cry to build.
